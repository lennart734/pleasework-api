💻 About PleaseworkAPI

Welcome to PleaseworkAPI — a simple REST API that receives AI-generated feedback for sleep quality and back pain management. Submit your structured JSON payload and let the AI handle the rest.

Base URL: https://pleasework-api.vercel.app
